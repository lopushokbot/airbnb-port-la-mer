How the money works now — posted-price era

The listing migrated to Airbnb's new fee structure in August 2026. Airbnb now charges a single 15.5% host-only service fee — guests pay no Airbnb service fee at all. That collapses the old two-sided pricing into one number: the price you set is exactly the price the guest sees, and you net posted × 0.83725. Every rate table below therefore shows both sides of the same posted price: YOU SET = GUEST SEES and YOU NET (×0.83725).

Worked example — three November nights at posted AED 1,100

  • Guest pays: 3 × 1,100 = AED 3,300 for the nights + AED 200 cleaning fee + AED 45 Tourism Dirham (AED 15/bedroom/night — the holiday-home "Deluxe" rate, not the AED 20 hotel rate) + 5% VAT. No Airbnb service fee.
  • You net on the nightly portion: 3,300 × 0.83725 = AED 2,789 — Airbnb's 15.5% host fee is the only Airbnb cut. Cleaning and Tourism Dirham pass through.

The old v4 semantics ("host rate + ~14% guest service fee ≈ ×1.22 on the guest side") no longer apply. Posted rates were raised ×1.155 in the migration so guest-facing prices — and cash netted — are unchanged. Competitor guest prices now equal their posted prices; the market fully migrates by Sep 15, 2026.
