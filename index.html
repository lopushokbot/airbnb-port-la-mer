The view — top tier, not the best

An honest read after comparing Sema's photos against eight other 1-bedroom Port de la Mer listings: this view beats roughly 80% of competitor listings. Most "sea view" claims in the development are marketing — actual balconies face internal courtyards or the opposite building's roof.

Sema's apartment is genuinely differentiated: marina water in the foreground, the opposite Port de la Mer arm in the middle distance, and the full Dubai skyline including a clearly visible Burj Khalifa across the water. The Burj Khalifa visibility is the real selling point.

  1. Edit the listing title. Change "La Mer Iconic View" to "Port la Mer · Burj Khalifa View · Designer 1BR" — bookers search by view + landmark, and Burj Khalifa is the true differentiator.
